Frequently asked questions

Urbest in a few questions

What is Urbest?
Urbest is CMMS and work request management software for organisations that manage one or more buildings. Occupants, technical teams and service providers report a request with a photo, track it and close it, on the web or in the mobile app.
Who uses Urbest?
More than 20,000 users in 8 countries: corporate facility teams, healthcare, public sector, retail, residential, and over 300 schools, including the high schools of the Normandy, Centre-Val de Loire and Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes regions.
Is Urbest suited to schools and regional authorities?
Yes. Teachers and staff report issues from their phone, technicians know what to do, and the business manager follows everything in one place. Urbest works for a single school as well as for a region running all of its high schools.
How is it different from an industrial CMMS?
An industrial CMMS is built around equipment and machine maintenance. Urbest is built around the flow of requests between occupants, managers and service providers, across one or more sites.
How do I get started with Urbest?
Book a demo: we answer your questions and set up your first building or your portfolio. A 30-day trial is included.
